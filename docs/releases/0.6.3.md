## Неподвижный safety contract

- deposit forecast is an approximation from the selected-month snapshot; maturity and rate changes are not modeled;
- T-Invest refresh remains explicit and owner-triggered; quantity changes do not cause background provider/network refresh;
- batch preview does not imply cross-position atomic Apply;
- no OCR, raw provider payload persistence, raw Alfa PDF persistence or persistent Alfa account mapping;
- Apply remains explicit; CLOSED/missing-month and statement provenance guards remain fail-closed;
- no cloud, auth, telemetry, trading or provider write operations;
- canonical Alembic head remains exactly `0029_statement_event_retract`; this task adds no migration;
- production remains local-only on `127.0.0.1:8000`.
