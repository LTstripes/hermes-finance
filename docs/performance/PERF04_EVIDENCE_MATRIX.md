# PERF-04P1 — Attribution evidence and capability matrix

## Purpose and boundary

This is a non-normative discovery record for PERF-04 at baseline
`858d64c4c6777c33cf21201095240536d258db7c`. It inventories persisted Hermes
evidence that a future attribution contract may inspect. It does not define a
return formula, choose an attribution convention, or claim that any row below
is itself an investment-return result.

The classifications in this document mean:

- **authoritative/exactly usable** — the persisted field has a direct identity,
  value and date/grain suitable for the stated evidence use, subject to the
  stated coverage conditions;
- **usable only for a narrower metric or period** — useful for a bounded
  monthly, account, instrument or event-level view, but not a complete
  attribution history;
- **incomplete/ambiguous** — some related evidence exists, but an identity,
  date, scope, currency or completeness fact is not guaranteed;
- **absent** — no persisted evidence or dedicated model was found in the
  current repository surface.

## Highest-value supported attribution opportunities

Within the evidence limits above, the strongest future attribution inputs are:

1. **Closed monthly account/instrument snapshots.** For a specific reporting
   month, account and instrument, Hermes can expose quantity, stored valuation,
   price date/source, cost basis and stored unrealized result, with account and
   instrument identity. This is a bounded snapshot view, not a continuous
   attribution history.
2. **Canonical owner-boundary events.** Valid `ExternalFlow` rows provide an
   exact account, event date, boundary amount, direction/kind, currency and
   optional resolved transfer identity. Their scope classification can be
   inspected without treating missing rows as zero.
3. **Explicit valuation-boundary observations.** Where `ObservedValuationPoint`
   rows are complete, exact, dated, provenanced and explicitly related to one
   flow or same-date group, they provide high-value pre/post boundary evidence.
4. **Event-level income and cost corroboration.** Investment cash-flow rows and
   statement-backed payout events can support bounded income, fee, tax and
   realized-P/L reporting, provided their event identity and limitations remain
   visible. Expected or provider-announced rows remain forecast/context data.
5. **Evidence quality and coverage diagnostics.** Scope membership history,
   cash-boundary coverage, in-kind coverage, quote provenance and stable
   fail-closed reason codes are useful for deciding whether a future consumer
   may use a particular evidence slice.

These opportunities are deliberately phrased as evidence reuse, not as a
normative decomposition or performance formula.

## Hard evidence gaps and Integrator questions

- **No general trade/fill/lot ledger:** Is a future attribution contract
  expected to introduce one, or will trade-level attribution remain explicitly
  unavailable? The current evidence cannot answer this.
- **No continuous valuation path:** Monthly snapshots plus explicit flow-boundary
  observations do not establish intra-period prices, quantities or valuation
  continuity. No interpolation or capital-delta inference is justified.
- **No general dated FX conversion evidence:** Currency labels are present, but
  cross-currency attribution needs an accepted conversion source, date and
  provenance contract.
- **Cash completeness is a separate assertion:** A clean flow table or closed
  month does not establish that all owner cash crossings were captured.
- **Historical scope transitions remain a boundary decision:** Effective-dated
  membership exists, but a portfolio composition transition has no persisted
  scope-transition valuation evidence. A future normative treatment must be
  accepted separately.
- **Income versus boundary crossing is not universally joined:** Actual income
  rows, statement events, expected payouts and external flows have different
  identities and semantics. They must not be merged by date/account alone.
- **In-kind movement coverage is not equivalent to valuation:** A recorded
  quantity movement or coverage assertion does not supply an event-date
  non-cash valuation when that valuation is absent.

The current repository therefore supports evidence-first, fail-closed discovery
for bounded monthly and explicitly observed flow-boundary slices. It does not
support an exact general attribution result across trades, lots, continuous
prices, missing cash crossings or foreign-currency conversion without further
accepted contracts and persisted evidence.
